# CCNA 200-301 v1.1 Resources

## Knowledge

- [Official: Cisco CCNA 200-301 v1.1 Exam Topics (PDF)](https://learningcontent.cisco.com/documents/marketing/exam-topics/200-301-CCNA-v1.1.pdf)
  The blueprint. Local copy at `reference/200-301-CCNA-v1.1-exam-topics.pdf`. Use for: deciding whether anything is worth studying. If it is not on this list, it is not on the exam.
- [Video: Jeremy's IT Lab, free complete CCNA 200-301 course (YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLxbwE86jKRgMpuZuLBivzlM8s2Dk5lXBQ)
  Widely regarded as the best free structured course; each video pairs with a free Packet Tracer lab and an Anki flashcard deck. Use for: primary knowledge source for every domain. Resource index: https://www.jeremysitlab.com/ccna-resources/
- [Book: _CCNA 200-301 Official Cert Guide Library_, Wendell Odom (Cisco Press, 2nd ed.)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/-/9780138221539)
  The only Cisco-approved self-study text; detailed, textbook style. Use for: authoritative explanations when a video leaves a gap; chapter review questions.
- [Book: _Acing the CCNA Exam_ Vol 1 & 2, Jeremy McDowell (Manning)](https://www.manning.com/books/acing-the-ccna-exam-fundamentals-and-protocols)
  Companion to the video course, tighter than Odom. Use for: reading alternative if Odom feels too slow.
- [Labs: Neil Anderson's free CCNA Lab Guide (Packet Tracer, 300+ pages)](https://www.flackbox.com/cisco-ccna-lab-guide)
  Complete free lab set with walkthroughs. Use for: extra reps on configure/verify objectives (2.x, 3.x, 4.x, 5.x).
- [Tool: Cisco Packet Tracer (free, via NetAcad)](https://www.netacad.com/courses/packet-tracer)
  Official simulator. Use for: every configure/verify objective. Not installed on this machine as of 2026-09-08.
- [Tool: Anki spaced repetition](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)
  Use for: daily retrieval practice with Jeremy's IT Lab decks. Not installed as of 2026-09-08.
- [Practice exams: Boson ExSim-Max for CCNA](https://www.boson.com/practice-exam/200-301-cisco-ccna-practice-exam)
  Consistently rated closest to real exam difficulty, with detailed explanations. Paid. Use for: weeks 10-12 readiness gauge; target 85%+ before booking.
- [Reference: PracticalNetworking.net CCNA index](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/ccna/)
  Precise, diagram-heavy explainers (ARP, subnetting, NAT, STP). Use for: clearing a specific misconception fast.
- [Curated list: Christopher Hart's CCNA resources](https://chrisjhart.com/ccna-resources/)
  Cisco TAC engineer's vetted list. Use for: second opinion on any resource before spending money.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ccna](https://www.reddit.com/r/ccna/)
  Large, well-moderated; weekly "I passed" posts with resource breakdowns and exam-day advice. Use for: study-plan sanity checks, what the real exam felt like.
- [Cisco Learning Network, CCNA community](https://learningnetwork.cisco.com/s/ccna-exam-topics)
  Official forum, moderated by Cisco; official exam-topic updates land here first. Use for: policy questions (retake rules, exam format, v2.0 transition).
- [Jeremy's IT Lab Discord](https://www.jeremysitlab.com/)
  Course-specific help. Use for: questions about a specific video or lab.

## Gaps

- No trusted free practice exam found that matches real difficulty; Boson is paid. Jeremy's IT Lab Udemy practice exams (2 x 100 q) are a cheaper alternative, unverified.
- Wireless LAN Controller GUI objectives (2.9, 5.10) are poorly served by Packet Tracer; need a source of WLC GUI screenshots or Jeremy's IT Lab WLC videos.
