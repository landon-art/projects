# Mission: Pass the Cisco CCNA (200-301 v1.1) by 8 December 2026

## Why
Earn the CCNA certification on the first attempt within three months of starting (start: 8 September 2026, target exam date: on or before 8 December 2026). The exam is the gate; everything taught here must map to a numbered objective on Cisco's official 200-301 v1.1 blueprint (see `reference/200-301-CCNA-v1.1-exam-topics.pdf`).

## Success looks like
- Score above the ~825/1000 passing threshold on the 200-301 v1.1 exam, booked at Pearson VUE for early December 2026 at the latest.
- Score 85%+ on Boson ExSim (or equivalent trusted practice exam) two weeks before the real exam.
- Subnet any IPv4 address/mask by hand in under 30 seconds, without a calculator.
- Configure and verify, from a blank Packet Tracer topology, in under 20 minutes: VLANs + trunks, inter-VLAN routing, single-area OSPFv2, static/default routes, NAT overload, SSH, and a numbered/named ACL.
- Read any `show ip route`, `show spanning-tree`, `show ip ospf neighbor`, or `show interfaces` output and explain each field.

## Constraints
- Hard deadline: 3 months. Exam v1.1 is reported to retire 2 February 2027, so the December target keeps margin for one retake on the same blueprint.
- Study budget assumed ~1.5-2 hours/day, 6 days/week (update in NOTES.md if different).
- Weighting drives time allocation: IP Connectivity 25%, Network Fundamentals 20%, Network Access 20%, Security 15%, IP Services 10%, Automation 10%.
- Hands-on labs in Cisco Packet Tracer (free) are mandatory, not optional: configure/verify objectives are ~40% of the blueprint.
- Prior networking experience: unknown at time of writing; lesson 0001 is a placement diagnostic to set the starting point.

## Out of scope
- CCNP-level depth (multi-area OSPF, BGP, EIGRP internals, MPLS).
- Vendor-neutral certs (Network+) and Cisco DevNet Associate.
- Building a home lab with physical hardware; Packet Tracer suffices for 200-301.
