Opening principles

Glossary

Terms used across every lesson. Lessons use these words exactly.

TermMeaning
OpeningRoughly the first 10 to 15 moves, until pieces are developed and kings castled.
CentreThe four squares e4, d4, e5, d5. Pieces there reach the most squares.
DevelopMove a knight or bishop off its starting square to a useful one.
Minor piecesKnights and bishops. Major pieces are queen and rooks.
Castle (O-O / O-O-O)King moves two squares toward a rook; rook jumps over. Needs: neither piece has moved, no pieces between, king not in check or passing through check.
Tempo (plural tempi)One move's worth of time. "Losing a tempo" means wasting a move, e.g. retreating a chased queen.
Hanging pieceA piece that is attacked and not defended. Free to take.
f7 / f2Weakest square for each side at the start: defended only by the king. Target of Scholar's Mate.
Scholar's MateCheckmate on f7 by queen and bishop, e.g. 1.e4 e5 2.Bc4 Nc6 3.Qh5 Nf6?? 4.Qxf7#.
ForkOne piece attacks two things at once.
NotationFiles a to h left to right from White's side, ranks 1 to 8 bottom to top. Nf3 = knight to f3. x capture, + check, # mate, ?? blunder, … before a move means Black's move.
