# Mission: Chess Openings

## Why
Stop losing games in the first ten moves. Reach the middlegame with a playable position every game, so that games are decided by play rather than by an early trap or a hung piece.

## Success looks like
- Reach move 10 in every game with king castled, both knights and at least one bishop developed, and no piece hanging
- Recognise and refute Scholar's Mate and other early queen attacks without thinking
- Play one reliable setup as White and one reply to 1.e4 and one to 1.d4 as Black, from memory
- Explain, for any opening move, which of the three jobs (centre, development, king safety) it does

## Constraints
- Assumed: online games (Lichess or Chess.com), rapid time control, beginner rating. **Unconfirmed, see NOTES.md**
- Lessons must be finishable in 10 to 15 minutes
- No deep opening theory memorisation; principles first, specific lines only once principles are automatic

## Out of scope
- Endgames, middlegame strategy, deep tactics training
- Memorising opening variations past move 8
