# Mission: Conversational Japanese

## Why
Hold a real conversation with my partner's family within six months (target: early March 2027). Not tourist Japanese: dinner-table talk with in-laws, and following what they say to each other.

## Success looks like
- Introduce myself to the family politely without freezing, and answer "where are you from / what do you do" questions.
- Sustain 10 minutes of dinner-table small talk: food, weather, work, how the day went, asking them about themselves.
- Follow the gist of the family talking among themselves at natural speed, and ask them to repeat or slow down when lost.
- Use the everyday politeness rituals (itadakimasu, gochisousama, ojama shimasu, etc.) naturally.

## Constraints
- Starting from zero (September 2026).
- About 1 hour per day available.
- Partner is a fluent speaker and will practise with me: every lesson ends with a real-world task to do with them.
- Listening comprehension is a stated goal, so daily listening input starts early.
- Script: learning hiragana in parallel (Tofugu says days, not weeks); lessons show kana + romaji until kana is solid. Kanji out of scope.

## Out of scope
- Kanji, reading newspapers, JLPT certification.
- Business keigo. Polite (desu/masu) form is the target register; casual form only for understanding the family, not for speaking to them.
