# Starting from zero, polite register, partner as feedback loop

User reported zero Japanese on 2026-09-08. Priorities are dinner-table small talk and understanding family members talking to each other, with about an hour a day and a fluent partner available to practise with. All lessons therefore target polite desu/masu speech, teach casual forms for listening only, and end with a task to perform with the partner.

## Implications
- Do not assume any kana knowledge yet; show kana with romaji until a learning record says kana is read fluently.
- Listening input should start in week 1 rather than after grammar, since comprehension is half the mission.
