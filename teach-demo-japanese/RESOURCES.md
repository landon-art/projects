# Conversational Japanese Resources

Verified 2026-09-08. Institutional and expert sources first; blogs only where nothing better exists.

## Knowledge

### Core curriculum (structured, polite register, everyday situations)

- [Irodori: Japanese for Life in Japan (The Japan Foundation)](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/)
  Official Japan Foundation coursebook, free PDF + MP3. Starter (A1, 18 lessons) through Pre-Intermediate. Built around real daily-life situations, polite form, lots of listening. Use for: the spine of the course; each lesson here maps to one Irodori situation.
- [Easy Japanese (NHK WORLD-JAPAN)](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/en/learnjapanese/)
  Japan's public broadcaster. 48-lesson audio drama with free PDFs and a podcast. Self-introduction is lesson 1. Use for: short daily listening with a transcript, weeks 1-8.
- [Genki I (The Japan Times, paid)](https://genki3.japantimes.co.jp/en/)
  Standard university textbook, polite-form first. Optional. Use for: if the user wants a paper book with exercises; otherwise Irodori covers it free.

### Grammar reference

- [Tae Kim's Guide to Japanese Grammar](https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar)
  Free, thorough. Chapters: Writing System, Basic, Essential, Special Expressions, Advanced. Teaches casual form first, so use as a reference, not a curriculum. Use for: looking up any grammar point a lesson touches.
- [Tofugu: A Guide to Japanese Grammar Guides](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/online-japanese-grammar-guides/)
  Comparison of free guides. Use for: picking a second reference when Tae Kim is unclear.

### Script

- [Tofugu: Learn Hiragana, the Ultimate Guide](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/learn-hiragana/)
  Mnemonic-based, reading-first, spaced quizzes. Use for: the parallel kana track, weeks 1-2.
- [Tofugu: Hiragana Mnemonics Chart (PDF)](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/hiragana-mnemonics-chart/)
  One-page cheat sheet. Use for: fridge.

### Meeting the family: etiquette and set phrases

- [Tofugu: Jikoshoukai, how to introduce yourself](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/jikoshoukai/)
  Structure, phrases, bowing, hand position, humility. Use for: first meeting (Lesson 0001).
- [LIVE JAPAN: Visiting a Japanese Home, 10 Etiquette Tips](https://livejapan.com/en/article-a0000693/)
  Omiyage and the phrase to give it with, ojama shimasu, shoes at the genkan, wait to be seated, offer to help clear up. Use for: the home-visit lesson.
- [Human Academy Japanese Language School: Words Used When Eating](https://hajl.athuman.com/en/column/000121.html)
  Itadakimasu, gochisousama, oishii vs umai, okawari, onaka ippai, with register notes (who says which). Use for: dinner-table lesson vocabulary.
- [Wikipedia: Customs and etiquette in Japanese dining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customs_and_etiquette_in_Japanese_dining)
  Chopstick taboos, seating, pouring drinks for others. Use for: what not to do at the table.

### Listening input

- [Comprehensible Japanese: Complete Beginner playlist (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPdNX2arS9Mb1iiA0xHkxj3KVwssHQxYP)
  Slow, gesture-supported, Japanese only. Site with level filters at [cijapanese.com](https://cijapanese.com/). Use for: 10 min daily from week 1; move to the Beginner playlist when Complete Beginner feels easy.
- [Linglass: Japanese comprehensible input index](https://linglass.app/comprehensible-input/japanese)
  9,000+ videos scored by difficulty. Use for: finding the next level once Comprehensible Japanese is outgrown.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Partner (fluent): primary feedback loop. Every lesson ends with a task to do with them.
- [r/LearnJapanese](https://www.reddit.com/r/LearnJapanese/)
  Large, well-moderated, strong wiki. Use for: "is this natural?" checks, resource questions.
- [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/learn/japanese)
  Free text/voice exchange with natives. Use for: low-stakes speaking beyond the partner.
- [italki](https://www.italki.com/)
  Paid tutors. Use for: weekly structured conversation from month 2 if budget allows.
