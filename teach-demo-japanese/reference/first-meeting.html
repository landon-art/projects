First Meeting Phrases

The fixed ritual for meeting your partner's family. Print this.

MomentSayRomajiMeans
Openingはじめましてhajimemashite"First time [meeting you]." Only ever said once per person, at the very first meeting.
Name〇〇と申します〇〇 to mōshimasu"I am called 〇〇." Humble, right for in-laws. Do not say watashi no namae wa.
Closingよろしくお願いしますyoroshiku onegaishimasu"Please look kindly on me." Closes any introduction; also used when asking a favour.
Entering their homeおじゃましますojama shimasu"Excuse the intrusion." Said stepping into the entrance hall.
Before eatingいただきますitadakimasuSaid together before the meal.
After eatingごちそうさまでしたgochisōsama deshita"That was a feast." Thanks the cook.

Body

  • Bow at hajimemashite and again at yoroshiku onegaishimasu. Speak, then bow; do not bow while speaking.
  • Hands at sides or in front (left over right). Never behind the back. Do not initiate a handshake; bow.
  • Name order in Japanese is family name first, but a foreign name in its usual order is fine.

Pronunciation rules that matter here

  • Every syllable gets equal time. ha-ji-me-ma-shi-te, six even beats. No stressed syllable.
  • The long ō in mōshimasu and gochisōsama takes two beats. Shortening it changes the word.
  • The final u in -masu and -shimasu is nearly silent: "mas", "shimas".
